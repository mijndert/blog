**The best thing that happened this week**

Since I made [CalAction free to use](https://mijndertstuij.nl/posts/making-calaction-free/) the number of daily downloads has tripled which just goes to show that 1. there's demand for an app like this and 2. that people actively despise the in-app purchase label. I'll be honest and say that I was not expecting this increase in downloads at all. But it's a very nice surprise and it makes me really happy to see people get value out of something I made. The fact that now more people have access to CalAction gives me way more satisfaction than the amount of money I was making off the in-app purchase. I think this is a good reminder that sometimes it's not about trying to make a buck, but about making something that's truly useful and valuable to people. And if it reflects well on me as a person, then that's a nice bonus.

At work it's been kind of busy as due to circumstances I had to take on a few extra responsibilities. I didn't really look forward to it, especially the IT support part, but I turned it around and made it into a personal challenge to see how fast I could clear the queue. And I did! The entire IT support queue is now empty and all my colleagues are happy with the support I could provide. Next week I can get back to engineering and setting out Q4-Q1 goals for my team. It's going to be another busy quarter with Black Friday coming up and I'm super excited to see what we can achieve as a team this time around.

**Playing**

I'm back to being absolutely obsessed with [Risk of Rain 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/632360/Risk_of_Rain_2/). I played it a lot in the past but I never really stuck with it. But now, for some reason, I can't get enough of it. Earlier I shared some of my [forever games](https://mijndertstuij.nl/posts/forever-games/) and that list shows I love a game that's easy to get into, but difficult to master. Risk of Rain 2 is exactly that. The utter chaos that ensues when you finally get a good run is like nothing else. I'm now trying to get as far as I can in Simulacrum which is a wave-based mode that gets progressively harder. Last time I tripped a mine at wave 58 because I couldn't see a single thing of what was going on. Just awesome.
